## Evidence and remaining checks

- **Hardware:** an assembled reader, four resolved channels, 58 dye acquisitions, explicit matrix/calibration comparisons and a documented assembly-feedback change provide concrete evidence. One feedback record is not an independent usability retest. Failed simultaneous-loading additivity limits mixture inference. Cell time courses do not establish a validated sensor.
- **Model:** the three studies connect calculations to physical design, decoder evaluation and receptor prioritization. Day holdout and matched comparisons strengthen the reasoning. Reproduction of the reported decoder remains incomplete without the KT corpus citation and matching training engine, fitted preprocessing, folds and seeds. Optical conditions differ between simulation and measurement, while docking/MD candidates remain computational hypotheses.
- **Engineering:** support Silver with an identifiable design decision, implementation, measured test, learning and resulting next design. Negative tests can inform that cycle; do not replace them with success language.
- **Education:** review the protected source evidence without rewriting it. Teaching resources and participant counts need documented reciprocal feedback and learning to substantiate award-quality claims.
- **Eligibility:** verify required forms, attribution completeness, actual elected awards, public wiki, presentation and session through the authorized team dashboard. Local files cannot establish these statuses. [Deliverables](https://competition.igem.org/deliverables/)
