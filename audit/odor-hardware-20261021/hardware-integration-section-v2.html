Living-cell integration

The four channels follow different trajectories after VUAA1. We compare the two ten-point series on the same initial blank reference, with the initial and final raw readings beside each trace.

D1 contained Or35a + ORCO, D2 Or85b + ORCO, D3 Or98a + ORCO, and D4 ORCO only. Three initial cells-plus-medium readings established F₀. We then recorded ten cells-plus-medium readings, added VUAA1 at 1:100 dilution, recorded ten more readings, and finished with three final readings.

Both lines use F/F₀: each measured amplitude divided by the same mean of Blank01–03 for that channel. The narrow bars show the initial and final three-reading means in mV, with every individual reading overlaid.

What changes during the readout?

D2 and D3 rise from low first post-addition readings toward the cells-plus-medium trace. D3 approaches that trace by the middle of the nominal window, while D2 remains somewhat lower. D1 decreases early and then stays below its cells-plus-medium trace. D4 shows an initial high reading, a dip and a recovery, ending close to the cells-plus-medium level.

The paired bars answer a separate question: how the three final readings compare with the three initial readings in raw units. They summarize the endpoint without replacing the time-course information. The distinct trajectories support further testing of the integrated readout, but this single run cannot separate receptor activation from handling effects, baseline drift or optical mixing.

Normalization, actual timing and measurement limits

F/F₀ = amplitude at a time point ÷ mean amplitude of Blank01–03. This is a ratio; no subtraction of 1 is applied. It uses the raw peak Fourier amplitude √(I² + Q²), not the magnitude of a blank-subtracted I/Q vector. The two lines share the same denominator, but their time origins are the first readings of their respective acquisition series.

The x axis follows the planned 6 s schedule: 0, 6, …, 54 s. Device timestamps show actual first-to-last spans of 79.43 s before addition and 69.19 s after addition. The last pre-addition and first post-addition records are separated by 144.96 s, and the addition event was not timestamped. The displayed nominal minute therefore does not specify the biological time elapsed after dosing.

The four frequency channels (41, 67, 89 and 113 Hz) share one photodiode; labels identify intended sample wells rather than independently unmixed fluorescence. Each acquisition contains 8,000 ADC samples at 4 kHz, with 25% LED duty. All 26 raw records passed the exported checks, had no ADC rail samples, and reproduced the saved amplitudes when recalculated.

ORCO only is a comparator. There is no matched vehicle-addition control or independent biological replication in this run. VUAA1 stock molarity, exact final solvent fraction and addition volume were not recorded. A stable baseline, timestamped additions, matched cell loading and independent repeats are needed before assigning these trajectories to receptor activation.
