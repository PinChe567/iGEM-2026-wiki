Living-cell integration

The assembled reader recorded four cell-containing wells before and after VUAA1 addition. The traces reveal a post-addition transient and baseline drift; they do not yet establish a receptor-specific calcium response.

We placed Or35a + ORCO in D1, Or85b + ORCO in D2, Or98a + ORCO in D3, and ORCO only in D4. These labels identify the intended wells. Each plotted channel is a frequency component measured by the shared photodiode, so its amplitude can include light from more than one well.

The device clock shows that the two ten-record series span 79.43 s and 69.19 s between their first and last midpoints. The last pre-addition and first post-addition records are 144.96 s apart; the exact addition time within this interval was not recorded. We therefore plot actual acquisition time rather than assigning an assumed response time after dosing.

A transient followed by partial recovery

D2 and D3 initially decreased after addition and then recovered toward their pre-addition levels. D4 showed an initial rise, then a decrease and recovery. Relative to the mean of the ten recent pre-addition records, the final three readings averaged −5.02% for D1, −3.82% for D2, −0.29% for D3 and +0.01% for D4. These are changes in measured electrical amplitude, not fluorescence ΔF/F₀.

What the integrated test establishes

The device acquired cell-containing-well signals across all four channels before and after dosing. All 26 raw recordings passed the exported acquisition checks, with no ADC rail samples, and recalculation from the raw samples reproduced the saved amplitudes.

The biological interpretation remains unresolved. This run lacks a matched vehicle-addition control and independent biological repeats, while the baseline drifts and the optical channels are not unmixed. The next test should log the addition event, establish a stable baseline, include vehicle-only handling controls, match cell loading, and repeat the experiment independently before assigning the signal changes to receptor activation.

Readout definition, controls and data record

The four channels use 41, 67, 89 and 113 Hz excitation, 25% LED duty, and 8,000 ADC samples at 4 kHz per acquisition. Raw amplitudes are √(I² + Q²), reported in mV peak. The saved browser correction subtracts an earlier blank I/Q vector and then takes its nonnegative magnitude; that magnitude cannot indicate whether a signal increased or decreased. A phase reference and valid living-baseline normalization were unavailable, so the analysis uses raw amplitudes without optical unmixing or concentration conversion.

ORCO only is retained as a comparator. The 1:100 VUAA1 dilution is operator supplied; stock molarity, exact final solvent fraction and addition volume were not recorded in this batch. Serial acquisitions describe within-run variation and do not provide biological replication or a significance test.
