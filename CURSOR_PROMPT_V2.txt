==================================================
1. CAMERA — STOP THE CONSTANT VIEWPOINT CHANGES
==================================================

The current camera changes angle too aggressively because it follows tangent changes and stage paths too literally.

Replace this with a stable chase-camera rig.

Rules:
- camera always stays BEHIND and SLIGHTLY ABOVE the fly/player
- camera forward direction should remain mostly stable
- do NOT roll the camera with every path turn
- camera roll max ±2.5 degrees
- fly bank may be ±16–20 degrees
- camera yaw response should be heavily damped
- camera pitch response should also be heavily damped
- use look-ahead smoothing, not instantaneous camera.lookAt(tangent)

Implement a camera target quaternion and slerp toward it slowly.

Use separate smoothing constants:
position damping ≈ 3–5
look damping ≈ 2–3
roll damping ≈ 2

Do NOT reset camera orientation abruptly at stage boundaries.

==================================================
2. PATH — SMOOTH, SWERVING, VERTICAL, BUT NOT ZIG-ZAG
==================================================

The player should actually need to steer.

Current problem:
if the player does nothing, they stay centered on the track and still score.

New rule:
there is a MOVING ODOR CENTERLINE independent from the master forward rail.

The master rail determines only forward progression / camera safety.
The ODOR TRACK oscillates laterally and vertically around that rail.

Concept:
odorOffsetX(u,t) =
  0.65*sin(u*2π*1.15 + phase)
+ 0.28*sin(u*2π*2.7 + phase2)

odorOffsetY(u,t) =
  0.28*sin(u*2π*1.6 + phase3)

Use smooth low-frequency movement.
Do NOT produce sharp zig-zags.

Player steering controls lateral offset.
Player can also visually rise/fall with path dynamics, but do NOT require separate up/down keys.

The track should pass left/right around obstacles.
