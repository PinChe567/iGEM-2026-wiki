Design rationale

AeroSense plans heterologous expression of selected Drosophila ORs with Orco in HEK293T cells so odorant or agonist exposure can be read as calcium-dependent GCaMP6 fluorescence. Published HEK insect-OR panels that combine Orco and GCaMP6 for VOC profiling motivate this architecture [1] Zboray et al., 2023 — full entry in References & Credits. . The points below explain why each proposal component is considered; they are design intent, not AeroSense results.

Component Why it is used (design intent) How it supports sensing or validation Citation / note
Selected OR CDS Provides ligand-tuned recognition diversity within the insect OR repertoire Defines which VOC / agonist responses the array can report once co-expressed with Orco [2] Robertson et al., 2003 — full entry in References & Credits.
Orco CDS Conserved co-receptor that partners with ORs in a cation channel complex Required for functional OR channels in heterologous sensing; also enables Orco-family agonist controls [3] Butterwick et al., 2018 — full entry in References & Credits. ; VUAA1 agonist literature [4] Jones et al., 2011 — full entry in References & Credits.
GCaMP6 Genetically encoded calcium indicator used to convert channel activity into fluorescence Planned optical reporter for agonist / VOC assays and hardware readout alignment [5] Chen et al., 2013 — full entry in References & Credits. ; heterologous OR panel context [1] Zboray et al., 2023 — full entry in References & Credits.
EMCV IRES + mCherry Proposal uses bicistronic OR and mCherry expression rather than an OR–mCherry fusion Planned transfection / expression check without forcing a fluorescent fusion on the receptor CDS Architecture from proposal; confirm against final map before claiming mechanism
GSG linker Short flexible linker proposed between GCaMP6 and Orco Intended to keep reporter and co-receptor as one fusion protein for co-delivery Proposal design; fusion orientation and linker length require map confirmation
CAG / inducible promoter Proposal separates constitutive OR–reporter expression from inducible GCaMP–Orco expression Intended to support expression control during validation; exact promoters pending map confirmation Proposal design; do not treat promoter names as sequence-verified

How future teams can use these parts

Until Registry IDs, sequences, and characterization exist, future teams should treat this page as design documentation only—not as a ready-to-order part package.

  • Prerequisites: institutional approval for HEK293T work and plasmid cloning hosts; access to appropriate containment; and a sequence-verified map matching any DNA ordered from this project.
  • Context dependence: OR/Orco function, GCaMP kinetics, and agonist responses are chassis-, promoter-, and assay-dependent. Published heterologous panels are starting points, not drop-in proof for AeroSense DNA [1] Zboray et al., 2023 — full entry in References & Credits. .
  • Recommended controls: vehicle; reporter-only or incomplete receptor combinations; and an Orco-family agonist control such as VUAA1 when ethically and institutionally approved [4] Jones et al., 2011 — full entry in References & Credits. . Exact concentrations belong in a reviewed protocol, not this inventory.
  • Known limitations: no AeroSense Registry IDs; no sequence files linked here; no quantitative AeroSense characterization; promoter/selection/fusion details still require map confirmation; optional OBP or microfluidic delivery ideas are out of scope until separately documented.
  • License / availability: wiki documentation on this site is intended for release under CC BY 4.0 when published. Biological materials, third-party plasmids, and Registry parts keep their own licenses or distribution terms. No AeroSense DNA distribution channel is claimed on this page.

Part-related contribution

A Bronze Contribution based on biological parts requires a genuinely reusable package: sequence-checked constructs or basic parts, maps, characterization summaries, and real Registry records with resolving links. Wiki scaffolding alone is not enough.

The candidate package tracked for that work is Biological construct and part documentation on the Contribution page. Its current status is In development: design intent exists; Registry IDs, sequence dumps, and reviewed part pages do not.

This Parts page will list real BBa_ IDs and Registry URLs only after the team supplies verified identifiers that match deposited sequences. Until then, no part-related contribution is claimed as Available.
