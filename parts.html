The parts collection

The design combines ten interchangeable sensing composites, one shared reporting module (BBa_26E11Z80, GCaMP6f–Orco), and supporting basic and reused parts (EMCV IRES, mCherry). Explore the architecture on Design and its expression evidence.

  • Sensing 10 composites Swap only the OR CDS / specificity module.
  • Reporting 1 shared module GCaMP6f–(GGGGS)3–DmOrco stays fixed.
  • Support Basics + 2 reused OR CDS parts, Orco, GCaMP6f, linker, IRES, mCherry.

mCherry is a QC / expression marker beside the channel, not the AeroSense measurement (Design · mCherry).

References

Research papers
  1. Sato, K., Pellegrino, M., Nakagawa, T., Nakagawa, T., Vosshall, L. B., & Touhara, K. (2008). Insect olfactory receptors are heteromeric ligand-gated ion channels. Nature, 452, 1002–1006. DOI: 10.1038/nature06850.
    Source highlights
    • Shows insect odorant receptors form heteromeric ligand-gated ion channels with Orco.
    • Core mechanistic rationale for OR–Orco sensing without a GPCR cascade in heterologous hosts.
    Back to citation
  2. Benton, R., Sachse, S., Michnick, S. W., & Vosshall, L. B. (2006). Atypical membrane topology and heteromeric function of Drosophila odorant receptors in vivo. PLoS Biology, 4(2), e20. DOI: 10.1371/journal.pbio.0040020.
    Source highlights
    • Demonstrates atypical membrane topology and heteromeric OR–Orco function in vivo.
    • Topology evidence used when placing reporters relative to Orco termini.
    Back to citation
  3. Butterwick, J. A., del Mármol, J., Kim, K. H., Kahlson, M. A., Rogow, J. A., Walz, T., & Ruta, V. (2018). Cryo-EM structure of the insect olfactory receptor Orco. Nature, 560(7719), 447–452. DOI: 10.1038/s41586-018-0420-8.
    Source highlights
    • Cryo-EM structure of the insect olfactory co-receptor Orco.
    • Structural context for the Orco channel in design discussions.
    Back to citation
  4. Chen, T.-W., Wardill, T. J., Sun, Y., Pulver, S. R., Renninger, S. L., Baohan, A., Schreiter, E. R., Kerr, R. A., Orger, M. B., Jayaraman, V., Looger, L. L., Svoboda, K., & Kim, D. S. (2013). Ultrasensitive fluorescent proteins for imaging neuronal activity. Nature, 499(7458), 295–300. DOI: 10.1038/nature12354.
    Source highlights
    • Introduces ultrasensitive GCaMP6 fluorescent calcium indicators for imaging activity.
    • Supports calcium / fluorescence reporting as an optical readout modality (contextual literature).
    Back to citation
  5. Roberts, R. E., Yuvaraj, J. K., & Andersson, M. N. (2021). Codon optimization of insect odorant receptor genes may increase their stable expression for functional characterization in HEK293 cells. Frontiers in Cellular Neuroscience, 15, 744401. DOI: 10.3389/fncel.2021.744401.
    Source highlights
    • Codon optimization can improve stable expression of insect OR genes in HEK293 cells.
    • Supports expression-engineering choices for heterologous OR panels.
    Back to citation
  6. Zboray, K., Toth, A. V., Miskolczi, T. D., Pesti, K., Casanova, E., Kreidl, E., Mike, A., Szenes, Á., Sági, L., & Lukacs, P. (2023). High-throughput ligand profile characterization in novel cell lines expressing seven heterologous insect olfactory receptors for the detection of volatile plant biomarkers. Scientific Reports, 13, Article 21757. DOI: 10.1038/s41598-023-47455-4.
    Source highlights
    • Heterologous insect olfactory receptors expressed in mammalian cell lines for odorant / volatile profiling.
    • Demonstrates a living-cell sensor route for plant-related VOC biomarker detection (literature context, not AeroSense data).
    Back to citation
