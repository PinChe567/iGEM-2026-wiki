AeroSense illustrated assembly manual
This manual explains assembly of the eight printed parts, PCB, optical seals, and gas tubes. Start with
assembly without cells. Date: 2026-10-08.
The English text follows ASD-STE100 Issue 9 writing principles. The Chinese text gives corresponding
procedures. Full vocabulary and rule compliance has not been audited. Each task separates preparation,
steps, checks, and corrective actions.
This is an engineering prototype. CAD dimensions are not measurements of the assembled device. The
nominal body measures 108 × 108 × 66 mm, excluding screw heads, tubes, and external filters. The four wells
share gas but contain separate liquids. The device has no cell-temperature, CO2, humidity, or automatic
gas-valve control.

4 Install the fan
Preparation: Use part 01, a 40 × 40 × 10 mm fan, two M3 × 16 mm screws, two nuts, and two washers.
Mounting pitch is 32 mm. The vendor must confirm the fan supply connection.
1. Put two nuts in the diagonal hex pockets under the base.
2. Put the fan in the four-corner support.
3. Point the fan airflow arrow toward the PCB.
4. Put one washer on each M3 × 16 mm screw.

6 Install the filter and light shield
Preparation: Use part 02, a qualified emission filter, and 0.8 mm opaque silicone sheet. Maximum filter size is
5.2 × 4.2 × 1.0 mm. Larger stock filters do not fit directly.
1. Invert the light shield on a clean tray.
2. Put the filter flat in the central filter seat.
3. Check that the filter does not obstruct the designed aperture.
4. Cut the PD seal with the full-size template.
5. Put the seal around the PD.
6. Check alignment with the actual PD window.
7. Turn the light shield to its normal orientation.
8. Lower the light shield slowly onto the PCB.
9. Check clearance around C21, R27, U8, and the LEDs.
The PD seal measures 4 × 4 mm outside and 3 × 3.4 mm inside. Nominal compression reduces thickness
from 0.8 to 0.6 mm. The modeled optical center is (0, -0.4) mm. Confirm its direction from the actual package.
The narrowest seal edge is only 0.3 mm. Incorrect cutting can obstruct the sensitive area.
Use the full-size seal template (../09_engineering_sources/CAD/drawings/gasket_cut_template_1to1.svg).
Measure a printed dimension before cutting. Resized Word figures are not cutting templates. If adhesive is
necessary, qualify it first. Apply it only at opaque corners. Keep adhesive off optical surfaces.
Check: The shield seats without screw force. Rigid prints must not press on LEDs or the PD. A green
transparent sheet is not a qualified emission filter. Verify excitation rejection and oblique-angle performance.

7 Install the wells and seal stack
Preparation: Start with empty wells. Use parts 04 to 07, part 08, the lower four-hole seal, and the upper ring
seal. The four well geometries are unchanged.
1. Insert the wells in their NE, NW, SW, and SE positions.
2. Point each well flange outward.
3. Check that all flat floors face downward.
4. Check that the four flange tops are level.
5. Install the lower four-hole seal.
6. Align its four holes with the well openings.
7. Put compression plate 08 inside the corner guides.
8. Install the upper ring seal.
9. Check all sealing surfaces for debris and wrinkles.
Lower seal: 20 × 18 mm outside, with four 4 × 2.8 mm holes centered at (±3.3, ±2.65) mm. Upper seal: 18.4 ×
15.6 mm outside and 14.8 × 12 mm inside. Each seal is 0.8 mm thick, with 0.2 mm nominal compression. The
plate is 1.2 mm thick. Nominal flange-top height is Z55.5.
Check: Each well lifts with light tweezer force. Nominal clearance is 0.15 mm per side. Do not omit the
compression plate. It distributes force to the well rims. Stop if flange heights differ.

Fill volume: The user reports that about 100 µL fills a printed well. Use 80 µL only as a provisional working
volume. First measure capacity with water away from the device. Then test lid closure. Reduce the volume if
liquid spills or wets a seal. The CAD estimate of 140 µL is not an operating limit. Existing 100 µL simulations
were not recalculated for this document update.

8 Connect gas tubes and close the lid
Preparation: Use part 03, two M3 × 35 mm screws, flexible couplers, PTFE tubes, and two hydrophobic gas
filters. Part 03 contains a horizontal shelf. No separate perforated plate is installed. All four wells share the gas
path.
1. Put the gas lid on a tray.
2. Identify the inlet side from the internal shelf.
3. Mark IN and OUT on the outside.
4. Support the root of the first printed tube tail.
5. Push a flexible coupler gently over the tail.
6. Connect the second tail in the same way.
7. Pass each tube through a side opening in the light shield.
8. Connect the inlet filter and sample tube.
9. Connect the outlet filter to the controlled downstream suction equipment.
10. Stop the suction equipment.
11. Keep the outlet open to gas flow.

Gas path: sample → inlet filter → IN → shared chamber → OUT → outlet filter → one controlled suction unit
→ approved exhaust. In CAD, IN is -X and OUT is +X. Do not identify ports only by left and right after turning
the lid.
Tail dimensions: 3.4 mm outside diameter, 4.0 mm retention ring, and 2.4 mm bore. The coupler fits over the
tail. Usable engagement is about 4.8 mm before the reinforcement rib. Do not force rigid PTFE tubing over
the tail. Side openings are about 7 × 7 mm. Leave enough tube slack to open the lid. Confirm the actual
coupler dimensions and material.
Check: Both ports are clear. The lid gap is even. Well rims are dry. The design calls for 0.2 µm hydrophobic gas
filters. A priced 0.22 µm reference filter does not establish sterilization or retention qualification. Confirm
intended use, pressure drop, fittings, and VOC recovery before substitution.

10 Replace wells and correct faults
1. Press Stop / LEDs off in the UI.
2. Check that acquisition has stopped.
3. Stop suction.
4. Keep the outlet open until pressure equalizes.
5. Set SW1 to OFF.
6. Remove the two lid screws.
7. Remove tension from the external tubes.
8. Lift the lid vertically.
9. Remove the upper seal.
10. Remove the compression plate.
11. Remove the lower seal.
12. Lift each well by its flange with tweezers.
13. Inspect for cracks, residue, deformed seals, and wet filters.
Corrective action: Disconnect power if liquid reaches the PCB. Follow the laboratory cleanup procedure
before reuse. If the lid does not close, inspect flanges, seals, guides, nuts, and tubes. Do not use longer screws
to force closure. Repeat calibration after changes to wells, fill, filter, cell batch, or duty.
For flashing and controls, use Operation_and_Setup in this folder. Report fit checks, measurements, and 
model predictions separately. Writing reference: ASD-STE100 official website.
