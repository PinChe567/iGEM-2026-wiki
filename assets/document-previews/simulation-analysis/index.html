AeroSense mechanical design and
evidence boundaries
Design freeze: 2026-10-05. This report introduces the mechanical decisions and then includes the complete
optical and airflow analyses. This is a traceable engineering-prototype package, not certification of physical
fit, cell compatibility or odor recognition. The PCB has not been manufactured; no fully populated STEP
model was available. A guaranteed first-print fit would therefore be an unsupported claim.
Current operating conclusion
The eight STL files are unchanged. The fixed-geometry flow sweep selects 3 mL/min as a conditional starting 
point: nominal worst gas t90=50.00 s; finer-grid check=50.98 s; half-timestep=49.85 s. This uses a provisional 
60 s / 90% inlet-concentration target, not a known biological threshold. Use one externally regulated 
downstream pump and physically verify flow; software settings do not measure or regulate flow. The 
protein-assisted capture mechanism remains unvalidated.
The UI now supports measured illuminated-blank I/Q subtraction, a measured fluorescence phase reference,
unsmoothed corrected export and timestamp-based display EMA. Raw data remain intact. Constant
subtraction cannot remove changing same-frequency contamination or shot noise. One cell-free
standard-liquid experiment tests hardware response and optical transfer; it does not establish odor sensing.
The later liquid/PDMS chapter is a conditional no-protein/no-mixing sensitivity, not an observed
response-time result.
Design decision
The user confirms bottom-adherent cells and100 µL per well. The transport analysis therefore includes the
approximately8.93 mm liquid column and evaluates a PDMS film at the well mouth. Retain the eight-part
experimental carrier and do not add PDMS to the selected assembly: hydrophobicity does not establish a
water-vapor barrier, and target-specific membrane properties are unknown. Gas arrival at the mouth is not
exposure at bottom cells. With stagnant liquid, assumed D=10 m²/s and an immediately fixed surface
concentration, bottom t90 is approximately22.83 h. This is a conditional transport warning, not a measured
biological response time. Assumptions, sweeps and limits are documented in the later PDMS/liquid chapter.
The nominal body is108×108×66 mm, excluding external tubing and cables. Its bounding volume is31.9%
below the previous114×109×91 mm envelope; this does not establish measured mass or battery endurance.
There are eight prints: four black structural parts and four independent flat-bottom clear wells. Two long
screws release the lid. A separate hinge, fan bracket, four-valve set and status LED have been avoided; the
phone reports status.
Each well is a single piece removed upward. Opaque sleeves, a minimum1.2 mm shared wall, the filter seat
and support arm are integrated into the hood. Only the Z42.5-44.2 mm bottom optical band remains open.
The well must not press directly onto LED or PD packages. Wells follow the LED positions in four quadrants;
their centre vectors are not exactly90° apart. The provisional PD sensitive-centre offset is(0,0.4) mm, distinct
from the package centre.

Optical-path and frequency analysis
Design decision
The new opaque carrier has nonzero optical collection and is suitable for a cell-free optical prototype. It has
not demonstrated complete well isolation, uniform illumination or sufficient biological detection sensitivity.
Wells are registered to the LEDs. The modeled PD-sensitive center is offset (0, 0.4) mm from the package
datum; assembly rotation and bottom-view mirroring still require vendor confirmation.
The carrier encloses the clear walls from Z 44.2 to 55.5 mm, with a minimum central opaque web of 1.2 mm.
The bottom band Z 42.5-44.2 remains open for excitation and collection. That shared band preserves the
optical path but also permits cross-well excitation. Actual black resin opacity at 470/520 nm has not been
measured. Retain the carrier, flat-bottom wells and PD seal; test filtering and reseating repeatability before
cell work. Do not remove the seal merely because the predicted signal increases.

Computation and checks
The primary sweep contains 24 conditions×3 seeds, 24,000 excitation and 60,000 emission packets per run: 
6,048,000 packets. Cell absorption events seed isotropic fluorescence with appropriate absorption weights. 
Power conversion is P_LED×absorbed_fraction×QY×470/520×collection_fraction.
Additional maps use 600,000 excitation packets and 224 isotropic source locations×65,536 packets. 
Collection means the fraction of source power reaching the PD, not peak-normalized brightness. Per-pixel SE 
and 95% Wilson intervals are saved. Median relative SE is 17.3%, with 0 zero-hit pixels. A zero-hit estimate is 
not proof of zero collection.
Sweep bars are mean±1 SE across three seeds, not 95% confidence intervals, and exclude 
material/biological/model uncertainty. With only three seeds, small rankings should not be interpreted as 
optimization proof.
Analytic rectangular solid angle, 4% normal-incidence Fresnel reflection, TIR, HG mean cosine and 10,000
random material-classification points passed. Maximum energy-ledger error was 2.9e-15. Primary runs cap
transport at 2500 events; maximum unfinished fraction was 0.0117%, with a worst conservative unresolved
green-power bound of approximately 0.121 nW in an individual run. Three additional higher-packet NE
comparisons at 2500/10000 events are saved in event_limit_convergence.json. This check addresses the
reference condition, not full convergence for every case.
Power and uniformity
NE reference green power is 0.593±0.035 nW; NW/SW/SE are 0.723/0.829/1.008 nW. These are LED-on 
powers. At 25% duty, time-average power is 0.25 times the on-state power and the sinusoidal fundamental 
amplitude is approximately 0.450 times it. On-state power must not be substituted directly for lock-in 
amplitude.
Mean upward NE floor irradiance is 4.366 µW/mm², with spatial pixel CV 46.2%. This is upward crossing flux 
per floor area, not all-direction fluence. It excludes cellular distribution variation. A flat floor does not imply 
uniform illumination. Side illumination, component shadows and refractive interfaces still create spatial 
structure.
Reversing PD offset gives 0.994 nW; centering it gives 0.922 nW. Removing the seal gives 1.103 nW, but
sacrifices the light/sealing boundary and is not recommended.

Is the intensity sufficient?
Nonzero collection is established in the model; odor detection is not. The relevant quantity is ΔF relative to 
measured noise. conditional_photon_budget.csv varies LED-on power 0.05-2 mW,QY 0.1-1,andΔF/F 1-50%, 
including the 25% duty fundamental factor.
At 500 µW,QY 0.6,andΔF/F 10%, predicted lock-in amplitude change is 16.03 pW. If SNR5 is chosen as an 
engineering target, repeated lock-in amplitude RMS noise must be below 3.21 pW equivalent input power. 
This is not a per-ADC-sample noise limit. Without measured responsivity, gain, filtering, baseline drift and 
GCaMP expression, it is not a defensible detection limit.

Custom double-precision 3D D3Q19 BGK lattice-Boltzmann implementation; screening τ=0.8, air ρ=1.2 
kg/m³ and μ=1.8e5 Pa·s. Halfway bounce-back on rigid walls and stationary liquid surfaces;

steps <2e5 and inlet/outlet mass imbalance <0.2%, for three consecutive reports. All 28 solves meet these 
declared criteria; maximum final imbalance is 0.0444%. C# versus NumPy tube-field relative L2 difference is 
3.09e-12. Hagen-Poiseuille tube-flow errors are 2.54% at τ=0.8 and -1.13% at τ=0.55. Numerical benchmarks 
are not experimental device validation.
Screening fields were solved at small pressure forcing and Stokes-normalized to 1 mL/min. Separate τ=0.55
runs actually produced 0.9400 mL/min (open) and 0.9401 mL/min (shelf); shelf Re=0.554, relative density

added gas-scalar model below still omits liquid dissolution, evaporation, moving liquid, cell injury, external

established. The current carrier is not thereby validated for rapid gas-to-cell delivery: the deep liquid column 
is an unresolved transport constraint that should be measured before making a rapid-response claim. 
Thicknesses in this study are a research grid, not alternative print files.
PDMS can be useful as a selective permeation layer under a maintained concentration gradient. It is not a 
universal VOC-only gate. A membrane that admits a compound still allows it to equilibrate in a closed 
receiver; without a sink, this model's final downstream gas-equivalent concentration equals upstream 
concentration. Selectivity primarily affects delivery rate, transient composition and retention, not permanent 
rejection at equilibrium. The molecular target, interferents, humidity, acceptable delay and recovery criterion 
must therefore be defined first.

Model and boundaries
For each layer, physical concentration is c = K u, where u is gas-equivalent concentration and K =
c_layer/c_gas at equilibrium, using the same mol/m³ concentration units. Gas has K = 1. The equation is K
u/t = (D K u/x)/x. Interface fluxes conserve mass and use the sum of both half-cell diffusion
resistances. Optional surface resistance adds 1/h at each PDMS interface; h uses gas-equivalent
concentration units, m/s.
The ordered layers are optional PDMS at the well mouth, gas headspace, and liquid. Sidewalls are
impermeable; the bottom has zero flux, representing an initially unloaded, non-consuming transport probe
at the cell position. Initial concentration is zero and the upstream concentration steps to one. The reported
bottom fraction is c_liquid/(K_liquid c_gas,input). Instantaneous partition equilibrium, dilute linear sorption,
constant D and K, uniform cross section, no liquid convection, no binding, no chemical reaction and no cell
consumption are assumed. A real surface meniscus, PDMS deflection, leaks and bypass around a film are
excluded.
The default input is a step immediately above the well rim. It omits upstream tubing, filters and plenum 
wash-in and is therefore favorable with respect to arrival delay. A separate sensitivity replaces it with a 
perfectly mixed 0.8544 mL plenum supplied at 0.1, 1 or 10 mL/min, with four identical wells drawing from that 
reservoir. That approximate volume is the rectangular plenum minus the shelf; ports are excluded. This is a 
reduced model, not the CFD flow field. couple_gas_history.py accepts a measured/CFD rim history; it is 
one-way forcing and does not recalculate gas transport after absorption changes the boundary.
The finite-volume system is transformed to a symmetric operator and propagated by its matrix exponential. 
There is no finite time-step error for step forcing. The last liquid-cell value approximates the impermeable 
bottom and is checked by spatial refinement. forced() integrates a piecewise-linear upstream history 
analytically in time. All governing units are SI; plotted hours and µm are converted from seconds and metres.

Intrinsic concentration permeability is P_c = D K in m²/s; bulk permeance is D K/L in m/s. Pressure-based
permeability is P_p = D K/(R T) in mol m¹ s¹ Pa¹. They must not be interchanged numerically. Intrinsic A/B

permeances together in this bulk model and does not itself change that intrinsic selectivity. Surface kinetics,
mixed sorption and swelling can break this simple result.

concentration peak after input has stopped. In the no-film example its peak is approximately 2.78% of the
input equilibrium reference near 3.92 h. The plotted storage/pulse curves demonstrate reversible diffusion

Oxygen, carbon dioxide and water
The oxygen calculation is a capacity ceiling under assumed steady, stagnant transport. It uses the cited 293 K
PDMS P/D, assumed liquid D = 2 × 10 m²/s, assumed surface dissolved oxygen 0.30 mol/m³ and gas
oxygen partial pressure 21,278 Pa. At 100 µL, maximum supply to a zero-concentration bottom is about 0.75
pmol/s per well; a 100 µm film barely changes this example because liquid resistance dominates. The demand
sweep 0.01-5 pmol/s is an assumed load, not a measured HEK293T respiration rate or a cell count. Above
capacity, the zero-order stationary solution is infeasible; negative concentration is not reported as physical.
This does not establish safe oxygenation, CO2/pH balance or a 37°C culture condition. Medium composition,
gas composition, cell number, respiration, mixing and target dissolved oxygen must be measured. Likewise,
water-repellent PDMS can still lose water vapor. No film is promoted as a moisture barrier, sterile barrier,
validated pressure seal or guaranteed analyte filter.
Numerical checks and the minimum next evidence
The 128-cell standalone membrane test reproduces L²/(6D) within 0.00305%; the liquid-bottom t90 error 
versus the exact series is 0.000762%. In the representative coupled model, increasing liquid cells from 48 to 
96 changes t90 by 0.00405%. The worst normalized mass-balance residual across the broad membrane 
sweep is about 2.17 × 10; all sampled concentrations stay within numerical tolerance of [0,1]. These checks 
validate the implementation under its assumptions, not the assumptions themselves. Full results are in 
results/validation.json, membrane_sweep.csv and summary.json.
Before adding a film, define one target VOC and one relevant interferent, the gas concentration units,
humidity, temperature, formulation and acceptable delay/recovery. Use the blank measurement templates to
record the actual film thickness and condition. Measure a no-film baseline and a film comparison with an
independent concentration assay; GCaMP alone cannot separate transport, receptor sensitivity and optical
effects. Measure the actual 100 µL bottom-delivery behavior first. A calibrated cell-free tracer can test liquid
mixing, but a different molecule does not establish target-VOC partitioning or membrane selectivity. These
are priorities, not an instruction to perform every point in the large simulated grid.
Keep the mechanical set unchanged while those data are obtained. If rapid bottom exposure is not achieved,
liquid-path redesign or verified mixing must be considered before presenting a rapid biosensor. Adding
PDMS without resolving that bottleneck is not supported by these results.
