AeroSense Results

HEK293 microscopy analysis

10 October 2026 · Pre-G418 expression screening · Original files preserved

Abstract

We imaged HEK293 cultures 44–48 h after transfection, before G418 selection, to examine fluorescence associated with the AeroSense expression system. The dataset contains 25 fields from 12 conditions, including an untransfected control, Orco alone and ten OR + Orco labels. Each field has brightfield, DAPI, green and red images. Using a conservative segmentation rule and a blank-derived fluorescence threshold, we identified 2,624 eligible DAPI objects across the dataset. RED-positive fractions in the OR + Orco conditions ranged from 0.7% to 16.7% within this selected subset. GREEN-positive fractions ranged from 0.0% to 7.0%.

Dense aggregates and overlapping blue signal prevented a reliable whole-population denominator. Eligible objects account for only 7.1–20.9% of segmented DAPI foreground among the OR + Orco conditions. These measurements therefore describe reporter-associated fluorescence around resolvable DAPI objects; they do not establish population transfection efficiency, viability, stable expression or odor sensing. All fields, thresholds, exclusions and measurement data are available below.

Introduction

AeroSense requires three distinct observations: delivery and expression of its genetic components, functional activity of the engineered receptor system, and reproducible responses to relevant odor stimuli. A fluorescence image addresses the first question only indirectly. The construct design assigns mCherry to the OR cassette and GCaMP6f to the calcium-reporting module. Red and green image channels are analyzed separately here, without assuming that a visible reporter proves correct receptor localization or channel function.

GCaMP6f is a calcium-sensitive indicator. Its resting fluorescence is not a constitutive, calcium-independent measure of transfection. Low green signal may reflect indicator state, expression, acquisition sensitivity or background, and a single endpoint cannot distinguish these explanations. Functional interpretation requires time-resolved, controlled stimulation. [1]

Our immediate question was narrower: which fields contain red or green signal above the blank background, and how much of the image permits an auditable object-level measurement?

The density and threshold choices materially affect interpretation

The images contain broad connected DAPI regions and clipping at the maximum exported value. Even a sharp edge around a fluorescent patch can contain several overlapping nuclei. Retaining a small, isolated subset avoids inventing divisions within those clusters, but introduces a selection bias. The excluded regions remain visible in every overlay.

Threshold choice also matters. For Or98a + Orco, the RED-positive fraction changes from 28.9% at the less stringent blank P95 threshold to 0.7% at P99. We use the conservative P99 rule as the primary analysis and report the full sensitivity table.

G418 selection is a separate experiment in progress

The team reports that G418 selection is ongoing in a different batch. No post-selection survival counts or fluorescence series were supplied with this dataset. The present images are therefore a pre-selection expression record, not evidence of enrichment by G418. A later G418 result should show selection duration and dose, the untransfected parental control, survival or growth counts, and reporter retention. Resistance alone does not establish both expression cassettes, stable function or an odor response. [5]

Methods

Source data and experimental context

The host was HEK293, as confirmed by the team. Imaging occurred approximately 44–48 h after transfection and before G418. The team fixed cells with 2% paraformaldehyde (PFA), performed no permeabilization step, and then stained with DAPI. The source workbook contains plate-layout and reagent-calculation records, including DAPI/G418 layouts and lipid-reagent mixtures. These records do not independently establish experimental execution or an assay outcome; the VUAA1/odor grids contain labels without response measurements. The workbook does not supply objective, numerical aperture, camera model, gain, acquisition software, DAPI concentration, fixation duration, or field coordinates. Neither the source TIFF tags nor the workbook establishes these missing acquisition details. The biological preparation and transfection record is linked from Experiments; undocumented settings are not reconstructed from a literature protocol.

Conservative DAPI segmentation

The BLUE plane was extracted from each exported TIFF. A 3 × 3 median filter was used for segmentation only, followed by a common threshold of >128 on the 0–255 export scale and 8-connected labeling. The bottom-right annotation rectangle (x ≥85% of image width and y ≥90% of image height) was excluded equally in every field. Eligible objects had area 200–2200 pixels, bounding-box aspect ratio ≤2.5, and object area/bounding-box area ≥0.4. Objects within 49 pixels of a frame edge or whose measurement neighborhood intersected the annotation exclusion were rejected. No watershed split was imposed on merged objects.

These rules define a conservative image-analysis subset, not a validated nuclear census. Segmentation has been visually inspected, but not benchmarked against independently annotated cell boundaries. Small touching nuclei or nuclear fragments can still pass these geometric filters, so an accepted object is not guaranteed to equal one cell. DAPI is a nuclear counterstain commonly used with fixed preparations. Here, fixation is confirmed and no permeabilization was performed; completeness and uniformity of nuclear labeling were not independently assessed. Fixed DAPI images do not recover the cells’ viability before fixation. [2]

Reporter measurement and positivity threshold

For each eligible object, the signal region contains its nuclear mask plus pixels within 12 pixels, assigned to the nearest blue connected component to limit sharing between adjacent objects. The local background is sampled 24–48 pixels from that object, excluding blue foreground; at least 100 background pixels are required. For RED and GREEN separately, the score is the signal region’s 90th percentile intensity minus the background median. Measurements use the original exported channel values, before the display adjustments.

This nearest-DAPI expansion does not segment cell boundaries. Excluding blue foreground from the background ring also does not exclude every neighboring cell’s cytoplasm. Fluorescent cytoplasm within that ring can raise the estimated background and reduce an object's score, particularly in dense fields.

The primary positivity threshold is the maximum of the three blank-field 99th percentiles of these object scores: RED >16.60 DN and GREEN >11.95 DN. DN denotes the 8-bit exported digital value. Alternative P95 and P99.5 blank thresholds are reported for sensitivity analysis. Thresholds are calibrated and evaluated using the same blank set, so the blank fraction is a calibration diagnostic, not an independent false-positive validation.

The reported fraction is 100 × positive eligible objects / all eligible objects. Condition summaries pool these counts across fields; they do not average percentages with different denominators. “Both” indicates both channel criteria within one object's measurement region. It does not prove two-plasmid delivery, protein localization, or co-expression in a viable cell.

Image processing and presentation

Original TIFF and workbook files were opened read-only and verified by SHA-256 checksums. New figures use fixed linear display windows across all conditions: DAPI 0–255, GREEN 0–120 and RED 0–180; brightfield remains 0–255. The enhanced display can clip bright exported values and is used only for viewing. Original-level views remain available. No sharpening, deconvolution, content removal, generative enhancement or reconstruction of missing detail was applied. The same-field RGB merges combine the original channel positions; no registration correction was imposed. [3] [6]

Web previews are resized with Lanczos resampling and saved as lossless WebP. The original scale bars remain within the full field; they were not used to infer pixel size. Object overlays mark classifications on a dimmed merge for legibility and have full-resolution PNG versions for audit.

Discussion and future work

What the images can establish

The dataset supplies a traceable pre-selection fluorescence record with negative controls. The object-level analysis detects localized signal above the selected blank threshold and exposes how strongly density and threshold choices constrain the result. It does not support a single reliable transfection-efficiency percentage for each complete culture. Fluorescent pixel area is also not a substitute for the number of fluorescent cells divided by a validated total-cell denominator.

Weak GCaMP6f signal

Green-negative objects cannot be classified as untransfected from these endpoints. GCaMP6f responds to calcium, and the optical state and expression level both affect the measured signal. The next functional experiment should report a baseline time course, vehicle controls and an appropriate positive response control, with the same acquisition settings and a transparent ΔF/F₀ analysis. A brighter display is useful for inspection but cannot create evidence of a calcium response. [1]

mCherry and cell viability

A red object is not, by itself, a live cell or a dead cell. Rounding, detachment, aggregates and strong fluorescence are insufficient to identify the cause here. No live/dead stain was acquired, so viability is recorded as unavailable. A spectrally compatible, independently validated viability measurement is needed to separate reporter expression from damaged cells, retained fluorophore or background. Common viability assays interrogate membrane integrity or cellular enzyme activity, which mCherry expression does not directly measure. [4]

Resolving the dense-center bias

A defensible whole-population estimate requires systematic field selection across the culture, a validated total-nucleus or cell label, and segmentation checked against manual annotation across both sparse and dense regions. Where two-dimensional images cannot separate overlapping cells, reacquisition at a resolvable density or an independent single-cell readout is preferable to drawing arbitrary boundaries. Report well- and day-level replicates separately from fields. More pictures from one culture improve coverage, but do not replace independent biological repeats.

For the current dataset, the immediate improvement is to retain all fields and their exclusions, complete the acquisition and staining metadata, and audit the object overlays. For the ongoing G418 batch, publish the selection trajectory with its own controls and repeat the fluorescence and functional assessment after selection. A matched selection series would test enrichment directly and give pre-selection and post-selection measurements comparable denominators.

Conclusion

The supplied HEK293 images contain reporter-associated fluorescence above the blank-derived threshold in a subset of resolvable DAPI objects. Dense overlap prevents a validated population denominator, and no viability or independent biological-repeat data accompany the images. The analysis therefore establishes a transparent expression-screening record and defines the next experiments needed to evaluate stable expression and receptor function.
