Experiments

How AeroSense was tested — protocols and assay parameters.

Methods for building, validating, and characterizing olfactory sensing cell lines. Construct rationale: Design. Iteration after failure: Engineering · Wet Lab. Wet-lab evidence: Results · Wet Lab.

The calcium-response assay was adapted from Zboray et al. [1] Zboray et al., 2023 — full entry in References. . AeroSense-specific construct, transfection, and validation procedures are documented separately below.

Protocol

Experimental workflow

One canonical protocol sequence. Bacterial propagation is part of molecular cloning, not a separate numbered step.

01

Molecular cloning

Prepare sequence-checked, endotoxin-free plasmids for mammalian transfection.

Key parameters

Parameter Value
Constructs Ten, specified as chemically synthesised and pre-cloned (GenScript)
Vector pcDNA3.1(+)
Host strain E. coli DH5α (Risk Group 1)
Bacterial selection Ampicillin 100 µg/mL
Culture medium LB broth (Miller), 25 g/L
Growth 37 °C, 180 rpm, overnight
Plasmid prep Endotoxin-free maxiprep

Method

  1. Verify sequences in silico before ordering. Check returned vendor sequences against the designs base-by-base.
  2. Obtain all ten constructs chemically synthesised and pre-cloned into pcDNA3.1(+) (GenScript).
  3. Propagate plasmids in E. coli DH5α under the bacterial conditions in the table above.
  4. Prepare DNA by endotoxin-free maxiprep. Keep DNA concentrated and low-salt.
  5. Verify both insert junctions by Sanger sequencing using the vector T7 forward and BGH reverse priming sites, plus internal primers for full coverage. Place one read across the (GGGGS)3 linker of the Orco construct so a repeat-driven deletion is not invisible.

Controls & critical notes

Use an endotoxin-free maxiprep. Keep DNA concentrated and low-salt.

Do not use PstI for diagnostic digests — most of the coding sequences contain internal PstI sites.

02

Mammalian cell culture

Maintain the mammalian host under defined growth, wash, and detachment conditions.

Key parameters

Parameter Condition
Cell line HEK293 (current protocol specification)
Growth medium DMEM (high glucose) + 10 % FBS + 1 % penicillin–streptomycin
Incubation 37 °C, 5 % CO2
Passaging At ~80 % confluence
Detachment TrypLE Express — 1 mL per T-25, 3 mL per T-75; 37 °C, 3–5 min
Wash before detachment Ca2+/Mg2+-free PBS

Method

  1. Grow cells in high-glucose DMEM with 10 % FBS and 1 % penicillin–streptomycin at 37 °C, 5 % CO2.
  2. Passage at about 80 % confluence.
  3. Before detachment, wash with Ca2+/Mg2+-free PBS.
  4. Lift with TrypLE Express (1 mL per T-25, 3 mL per T-75; 37 °C, 3–5 min).

03

Transfection

Deliver OR and Orco plasmids into mammalian cells.

Key parameters

Parameter Value
Current method in this specification Electroporation in suspension (not adherent)
Cells per reaction 1–5 × 106, log phase, > 90 % viability
DNA per reaction ~5 µg total. OR plasmid : Orco plasmid DNA mass ratio 2:1 (not protein stoichiometry)
Buffer Low-conductivity electroporation/nucleofection buffer, 100 µL
Recovery Pre-warmed DMEM + 10 % FBS, no antibiotics

Method

Transfect cells in suspension, not adherent, using the DNA mass, plasmid DNA mass ratio, buffer volume, and recovery medium in the table. Pulse voltage, pulse number, instrument programme, cuvette, and named buffer are not specified in this source.

Zboray et al. used FuGene HD into HEK293 stable lines [1]. That is not substituted for the AeroSense transfection specification above.

Controls & critical notes

  • Buffer-only pulse — electroporation toxicity.
  • GFP-only plasmid — transfection efficiency.
  • Genetic control Orco–GCaMP6f alone, without a tuning OR — should respond to VUAA1 but not to VOCs.

Use endotoxin-free, low-salt, concentrated DNA. Use healthy log-phase cells and minimise time in electroporation buffer.

Recover immediately in warm antibiotic-free medium. Omit penicillin–streptomycin during electroporation and for the following 24 h.

04

Stable-line selection

Select G418-resistant mammalian lines after transfection.

Key parameters

Stage G418 concentration Duration
Selection start (24–48 h post-transfection) 100 µg/mL 2 weeks
Selection raised 150 µg/mL 1 week
Maintenance ~75 µg/mL ongoing

Method

  1. Before transfection, run a G418 kill curve on untransfected host cells (draft range 0–800 µg/mL) for that cell lot, confluence, and G418 batch. Do not treat the table below as a confirmed effective dose.
  2. After transfection, allow 24–48 h recovery. Do not add G418 at seeding.
  3. Start selection at 100 µg/mL G418 for 2 weeks. Refresh selection medium every 2–3 days.
  4. Raise G418 to 150 µg/mL for 1 week.
  5. Maintain lines at ~75 µg/mL G418 thereafter.

During the first selection window, little visible effect is expected for 1–2 days, bulk cell death at days 3–5, and discrete resistant colonies against a cleared background by days 10–14. Those timings are operational expectations, not recorded AeroSense outcomes.

Controls & critical notes

  • Negative control Untransfected well at the same G418 dose throughout selection — every cell in it should die.

Add G418 24–48 h after transfection, not at seeding, so cells can recover and express NeoR.

The 100 → 150 → ~75 µg/mL schedule is a current protocol target / literature-informed starting condition, not an experimentally confirmed AeroSense kill curve.

05

Expression / functional validation

Confirm cassette transcription and Orco/GCaMP function before VOC assays.

Key parameters

Parameter Value
mCherry microscopy ex ~587 nm / em ~610 nm
GCaMP6f rest fluorescence ex ~485 nm / em ~510 nm; dim at rest is expected, not a failure
VUAA1 Synthetic Orco agonist used as a functional positive-control stimulus [2]
Western blot Optional / implementation details pending
Ionomycin Optional / implementation details pending

Method

  1. Record mCherry (ex ~587 nm / em ~610 nm) versus mock / untransfected cells. Red fluorescence reports that the OR cassette is transcribed. It does not report OR folding or plasma-membrane trafficking.
  2. After an expression gate, compare vehicle versus VUAA1 on cells that should contain Orco–GCaMP6f. VUAA1 is a synthetic Orco agonist used as a functional positive-control stimulus [2] Jones et al., 2011 — full entry in References. . A VUAA1 response supports functionality of the Orco/GCaMP sensing chain. It does not, by itself, prove membrane trafficking.

Controls & critical notes

  • Expression control Mock / untransfected cells for mCherry autofluorescence.
  • Functional positive control VUAA1 versus vehicle. VUAA1 is a synthetic Orco agonist used as a functional positive-control stimulus, not a proof of membrane trafficking.
  • Optional / implementation details pending Ionomycin (reporter isolated from channel) and anti-His western blot of the RSET leader. Concentration, solvent, incubation, antibody, and whether either assay is in the AeroSense SOP are not specified.

GCaMP6f is dim at rest. Absence of bright green is expected, not a failure.

06

Calcium imaging assay

Measure VOC-evoked GCaMP6f fluorescence in a 384-well plate with a matched vehicle injection.

The calcium-response assay was adapted from Zboray et al. [1] Zboray et al., 2023 — full entry in References. . AeroSense-specific construct, transfection, and validation procedures are documented separately above. Plate geometry, buffer volumes, and the two-round injection sequence below follow that published assay design.

Key parameters

Parameter Value
Plate format Black-wall, clear-bottom 384-well
Seeding 12,000–18,000 cells per well, 48–72 h before measurement
Assay volume 20 µL assay buffer per well
Vehicle injection 7 µL plain VOC buffer
Odorant injection 9 µL of VOC at 4× final concentration
Final well volume 36 µL (20 + 7 + 9)
VOC concentrations 1, 10 and 100 µM final
VUAA1 reference 50 µM on every plate for every cell line
Instrument Multimode plate reader with a two-channel injector (model not specified)
Readout ex 485 nm / em 535 nm
Sampling F0 for 5 s; then 30 s at 1 read/s after each injection

Buffers

Component Assay buffer VOC buffer
NaCl140 mM140 mM
KCl5 mM5 mM
CaCl22 mM2 mM
MgCl21 mM1 mM
HEPES-Na5 mM5 mM
Glucose20 mM31 mM
DMSO—0.1 %
pH / osmolarity7.3 / 340 mOsm7.3 / 340 mOsm

Method

Read on a multimode plate reader with a two-channel injector, ex 485 nm / em 535 nm.

  1. Seed cells at 12,000–18,000 per well in black-wall, clear-bottom 384-well plates, 48–72 h before measurement.
  2. On the day of measurement, replace growth medium with 20 µL assay buffer per well.
  3. Round 1 — vehicle: record baseline F0 for 5 s, inject 7 µL plain VOC buffer, record 30 s at 1 read/s.
  4. Round 2 — odorant: into the same wells, inject 9 µL of VOC at 4× final concentration, same recording protocol.
  5. Layout: one compound per row; up to eight cell lines per 24-column plate. Use three wells per cell line and measure each VOC–OR pair on at least three separate days.

The volumes are self-consistent: 20 + 7 = 27 µL, then + 9 = 36 µL; 9/36 = 1/4, so a 4× stock dilutes exactly to 1× final. The vehicle injection simultaneously establishes the volume for the second round and measures the solvent and injection artefact alone.

Reagent preparation VUAA1 stock and VOC handling

VUAA1. Dissolve in DMSO to a 50 mM stock (18.4 mg/mL). Aliquot and store at −20 °C protected from light. Prepare the 4× working dilution fresh in VOC buffer.

VOCs. Dilute directly in VOC buffer, or first in DMSO for water-insoluble compounds. Prepare working solutions fresh daily. Store concentrated stocks in amber glass with PTFE-lined caps.

Controls & critical notes

  • Vehicle control Round-1 injection of plain VOC buffer — solvent and injection artefact.
  • Normalisation reference 50 µM VUAA1 for each cell line on the same plate/day. Normalise to the mean VUAA1 response of that line, not a same-well VUAA1 injection.
  • Specificity control Ca2+-free condition. Zboray et al. showed that Ca2+-free and Ca2+-free + EGTA buffers abolish the VUAA1 response [1].

Keep DMSO identical in VOC, vehicle, and VUAA1 wells.

CaCl2 in the assay buffer is required.

Use HEPES-Na rather than bicarbonate: the plate sits in ambient air.

Reproducibility metadata

Technical replicates
Three wells per cell line, one compound per row, triplicate
Day replicates
Each VOC–OR pair measured on at least three separate days
Instrument
Multimode plate reader with a two-channel injector (model not specified)
Excitation / emission
485 nm / 535 nm
Sampling rate
1 read/s for 30 s after each injection; F0 recorded for 5 s before injection
Analysis
R then Rnorm; see Data analysis

07

Data analysis

Convert raw fluorescence into a VUAA1-normalised relative response for each receptor–stimulus pair.

Relative-response definition, the ≤ 0.05 floor, plate/day VUAA1 mean normalisation, and Grubbs’ outlier removal follow Zboray et al. [1] Zboray et al., 2023 — full entry in References. . Zboray et al. denote the relative response R as ΔF. This page uses R and Rnorm to avoid mixing ΔF with ΔF/F0.

Key parameters

Parameter Value
Raw fluorescence Plate-reader intensity at ex 485 nm / em 535 nm
F0 Baseline recorded for 5 s before the relevant injection
Fmax Peak fluorescence in the post-injection window
Raw relative response R = (Fmax − F0) / F0
VUAA1-normalised response Rnorm = RVOC / mean(RVUAA1, corresponding cell line, same plate/day)
Zero floor Rnorm ≤ 0.05 treated as zero, following [1]
Outliers Removed by Grubbs’ test, following [1] (α not specified here)

Method

  1. Record raw fluorescence at ex 485 nm / em 535 nm. Take F0 as the 5 s baseline before the relevant injection.
  2. Take Fmax as the peak in the post-injection window. Compute R = (Fmax − F0) / F0.
  3. Normalise each VOC response to the mean 50 µM VUAA1 response of the corresponding OR cell line measured on the same plate/day: Rnorm = RVOC / mean(RVUAA1, corresponding cell line, same plate/day). Do not use a same-well VUAA1 injection as the denominator.
  4. Following [1], treat Rnorm ≤ 0.05 as zero, and remove outliers by Grubbs’ test.

Controls & critical notes

VOC responses are normalized to the mean 50 µM VUAA1 response of the corresponding OR cell line measured on the same plate/day. Do not use a same-well VUAA1 injection as the denominator.

Reproducibility metadata

Excitation / emission
485 nm / 535 nm
Sampling
F0 for 5 s before the relevant injection
Analysis method
R, then plate/day mean 50 µM VUAA1 Rnorm; ≤ 0.05 floor and Grubbs’ test following [1]

Safety

All work is contained. E. coli DH5α is Risk Group 1 (BSL-1); HEK293 (the mammalian line named in this protocol specification) is handled at BSL-2 in a Class II biosafety cabinet. Aromatic VOC ligands are flammable and toxic and are handled in a fume hood per their Safety Data Sheets. Nothing is released outside the laboratory.

Full biosafety, chemical handling, and training records are on the Safety & Security page.
