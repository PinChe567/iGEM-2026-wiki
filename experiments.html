Status legend

Statuses are assigned manually from team evidence. They are never auto-assigned from the calendar or proposal milestones.

  • Planned — protocol drafted; not yet evidence of execution.
  • Pilot completed — limited run documented in a dated notebook entry; not a full study claim.
  • Completed — full planned run finished with notebook evidence and linked outputs.
  • Repeated — independent repetition documented after a prior completed or pilot run.
  • Internally verified — a second team member reviewed the record and confirmation is dated.

A protocol may move above Planned only when a dated Notebook link exists. Future milestones are not experiment dates.
