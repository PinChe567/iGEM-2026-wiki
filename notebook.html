How records are classified

Class Meaning Where it appears
Planned milestone A schedule or phase intention from the proposal or team plan — not proof of execution Planned milestones only
Work performed Dated actions that were actually carried out Actual notebook entries
Observation What was seen or measured, without overinterpretation Inside an actual entry
Decision A documented choice that changes constructs, protocols, models, or hardware Actual entry and/or Change log
Deviation Departure from a written protocol or plan, with consequence noted Inside an actual entry
Follow-up Next action assigned after an entry Inside an actual entry

Actual notebook entries

Entries are listed in reverse chronological order (newest first) when dated records exist. Every real entry must include a machine-readable ISO date on a <time> element, contributors, workstream, linked experiment or Engineering cycle ID, objective, work performed, observation summary, deviations, evidence links, decision/next action, and reviewer/status.
