Responsible use of AI

AI assisted website development, editing, code-based analysis and conceptual illustrations. Codex supported website revisions and analysis scripts; OpenAI image generation produced editorial artwork. A team-supplied Claude review informed scientific and usability revisions.

The team reports using ChatGPT-6 Astra, Claude Opus 5.5 and Fable 5.1. NTHU team members manually reviewed the AI-assisted content, analyses and code. These model names are supplied by the team; image-specific credits identify the generation tool recorded for each asset.

Scientific review

Generated artwork explains concepts. Experimental claims are based on the underlying measurements. The data package retains raw files, analysis choices and exclusions for review. Team members checked the analyses against their source data; the relevant Discussion sections describe the scope of interpretation.

The team retains responsibility for scientific claims, source permissions, attributions and the final submission. Source provenance and the scope of validation accompany the corresponding evidence.

Image provenance
