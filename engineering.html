Engineering Success

Traces decisions, tests, failures, and design changes across AeroSense subsystems.

Silver Engineering Success requires at least one real Design → Build → Test → Learn iteration with evidence. A proposal schedule or generic DBTL description is not enough. Cycles without recorded tests stay planned or incomplete.

No complete evidenced cycle published yet

How to read this page

  • Design — requirement and rationale for a changeable technical choice.
  • Build — construct, protocol, code, or hardware that was actually implemented.
  • Test — controls, measurements, and a link to real evidence (figure, notebook, dataset, protocol, or hardware record).
  • Learn — interpretation of the test, including failure or uncertainty.
  • Decision / Change — what the team changed next, or why no change was made.
  • Evidence — a resolving link to primary records. Placeholder wiki scaffolding alone is not evidence.
  • Status — Planned, Incomplete, In progress, or Complete (complete only when Design through Change are evidenced).

This page is distinct from Contribution: Engineering documents AeroSense’s own iterations; Contribution documents packages reusable by others.

Strongest complete iteration · content needed

No fully evidenced Design → Build → Test → Learn cycle is available yet

Judges looking for a complete Silver Engineering Success story will not find one on this page today. Results, Notebook, and Experiments are still scaffolding and do not contain dated test records, figures, or datasets that close a cycle.

When the team supplies one complete cycle, place it here first: Design question, Build artifacts, Test with evidence links, Learn (including failure), and the concrete Change that followed.

  • Required: implemented Build (not only a plan)
  • Required: Test evidence link that resolves to a primary record
  • Required: Learn that states what changed next, or why nothing changed
  • Forbidden: inventing a neat cycle from the proposal schedule

Biological sensing cycles

Planned design questions for the living-cell track. Build, Test, Learn, and Change remain incomplete until team records exist. Literature that informs the design intent includes heterologous insect OR panels in mammalian cells [1] Zboray et al., 2023 — full entry in References & Credits. and VUAA1 as an Orco-family agonist for functional controls [2] Jones et al., 2011 — full entry in References & Credits. . Citations are not AeroSense test evidence.

Cycle C1 · Living-cell sensor

Status Planned

  1. Design

    Requirement: Co-express selected Drosophila OR(s) with Orco and a GCaMP6 reporter in HEK293T so odorant or agonist exposure can be read optically.

    Rationale: Establish a living sensor element whose function can be tested with genetic and vehicle controls before VOC panel work.

  2. Build

    Incomplete — record constructs, cloning steps, or transfection batch actually implemented.

  3. Test

    Incomplete — controls, measurements, and primary evidence required.

    Planned evidence anchors (no primary records yet): EXP-AS-01, EXP-AS-02, Notebook · EXP-AS-01, Results · EXP-AS-01.

  4. Learn

    Incomplete — interpretation, failure, or uncertainty after a real test.

  5. Change

    Incomplete — state the next design change and why, or why no change was made.

Integration cycle
