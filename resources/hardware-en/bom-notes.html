Budget note 3

These are budgets, not an order-ready total. The device includes USD 377.30 for a custom thin emission filter. Edmund 67-016 lists at USD 284, but its 12.5 mm diameter and approximately 2 mm thickness do not fit the 5.2 × 4.2 × 1.0 mm seat. Cutting the outline alone does not fix thickness. Obtain a dimensional, angular-spectrum and fabrication quotation. LEE 101 stacks are an experimental candidate in the optical comparison; their price and installed compatibility are not yet substituted for this allowance.
