Gas-delivery reference and model boundary

The current source evaluates sample-gas delivery through the chamber. The electronics fan is not a calibrated sample-gas pump. The candidate 3 mL/min operating point is conditional on the modeled geometry, inlet flow, molecular diffusivity and a passive gas-interface probe.

A 4 × 2.8 × 12.5 mm cavity has nominal volume 140 µL. The historical model uses 100 µL and leaves 3.571 mm below the rim, but the team reports that 100 µL fills the physical well. Resolve this discrepancy with dimensions and at least three gravimetric fills before adopting the simulated flow. The proposed 80 µL handling volume has not been resimulated.
