# Archived activity plan — AeroSense QC Shift

**Status:** Archived QC Shift teaching plan and item definitions. This document does not describe the current Game 2.

**Current Game 2:** `games/labyrinth/index.html` opens **AeroSense: Scentbound Labyrinth (Odor Maze)**. The current entry point starts `startScentbound`; players explore a maze using illustrative scent-vision scanning. It does not mount the optional education study mode. Adding `?study=1` does not enable QC Shift or the G2 questions below.

**Archived teaching purpose:** Read an early-warning signal, manage uncertainty, and decide when a food batch needs retesting or confirmation.  
**Approximate duration (planned, not measured):** 8–12 minutes.  
**Planned levels:** Junior, Standard, Challenge.

## Learning concept

**Screening is not confirmatory diagnosis.** Invalid or uncertain measurements need appropriate follow-up (retest / hold-and-confirm), not blind trust and not an automatic “destroy everything” rule.

## Archived activity design

1. Choose a shift length / difficulty.
2. Short tutorial (first visit).
3. For each simulated batch: **monitor**, **retest**, or **hold & confirm**, with limited time and tokens.
4. Keyboard: `1` monitor, `2` retest, `3` hold & confirm, Enter to continue.

The archived design specifies risk and signal quality as **numbers plus glyphs**, not color alone. These instructions are not controls for the current Maze.

## Science note (illustrative)

QC Shift was designed as a **role-play screening shift**. Its proposed alerts and outcomes are simulations, not warehouse SOPs, toxin limits, or AeroSense hardware performance.

The current Maze has its own in-game **Science** note. That note does not make the archived QC Shift assessment a matched instrument for Maze play.

## Archived evidence schema

Monitor / retest / hold-confirm counts, missed simulated high-risk lots, unnecessary holds, invalid-reading follow-up, optional alert preference.

These fields describe the archived QC Shift simulation. Their presence in the shared data schema is not evidence of a current Maze export. Do not treat them as professional QC metrics or reinterpret them as Maze outcomes.
