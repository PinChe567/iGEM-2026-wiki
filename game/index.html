HOW TO PLAY

How to play

  1. Study six odorsThe game selects 6 odors and shows each image with its base pattern.
  2. Observe the patternThe challenge shows the pattern; purple lights are random noise.
  3. Choose the odorChoose the correct image from four options across 10 questions.
  4. Reach 60%Answer at least 6 questions correctly to pass. A perfect run earns 1,000 points.

Why do similar odors have similar patterns?
Each cell has different sensitivity to illustrative fruity, floral, fresh, roasted and savory features. Similar feature vectors share more active cells.
