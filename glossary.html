127 definitions

001

ADC

Analog-to-digital converter — hardware that samples an analog voltage and encodes it as a digital value.

002

Aflatoxin

A family of mycotoxins produced by some Aspergillus fungi. Detecting an odor associated with a fungus does not measure toxin concentration.

003

AL

Antennal lobe — the insect brain center that receives primary olfactory input and performs early odor processing.

004

ALI

Air–liquid interface — a culture or sampling setup where cells contact gas above a liquid medium.

005

ANOVA F score

A statistical score comparing variation between groups with variation within groups; used here as one feature-ranking check.

006

B2B

Business-to-business: a product or service sold to an organization rather than directly to individual consumers.

007

Baseline model

A simple comparison method used to judge whether a more complex model actually adds value.

008

Beachhead market

The first narrowly defined customer group and use case a team chooses to test before expanding.

009

BioBrick

A standardized genetic part format used in iGEM and related registries for modular DNA assembly.

010

BOM

Bill of materials — the list of parts, quantities, and specifications needed to build a device.

011

BsaI

A type IIS restriction enzyme used in some DNA assembly workflows; an internal site can interfere with that assembly method.

012

BSL

Biosafety level — a set of containment practices and facilities matched to the organisms and procedures in a laboratory.

013

CAD

Computer-aided design — the digital geometry and drawings used to describe and revise a physical part.

014

Calibration

Comparing an instrument with a known reference so its response can be interpreted or corrected.

015

CDS

Coding sequence — the DNA segment whose information is translated into a protein.

016

CFD

Computational fluid dynamics — numerical calculations of fluid motion, used here to compare airflow and pressure through the reader.

017

Clipping

A signal exceeding the usable measurement range and being cut off at a limit. Background subtraction cannot recover the missing signal.

018

CMV promoter

A promoter derived from cytomegalovirus that is commonly used to drive gene expression in mammalian cells.

019

Codon optimization

Changing a gene's DNA codons while preserving its protein sequence, often to suit the expression host.

020

Composite part

A genetic construct assembled from multiple functional DNA elements to perform a defined role.

021

Confirmatory testing

A follow-up test using an appropriate validated method to investigate a screening signal.

022

Confusion matrix

A table comparing predicted classes with reference labels, including correct and incorrect decisions.

023

Cross-talk

Signal from one channel appearing in another. In the four-well reader, optical mixing can remain even after frequency-selective processing.

024

Cross-validation

Evaluating a model across multiple training/test splits to estimate how it performs on unseen data.

025

Dark noise

Electrical output from a light-detection system even when no intended light reaches its sensor.

026

Data leakage

Information from the test set enters model training, making performance appear better than it would be on truly new data.

027

DBTLR

Design → Build → Test → Learn → Redesign: a cycle that records a decision, its test, what was learned, and the next change.

028

DDS

Direct digital synthesis — generating a periodic waveform using a digital phase accumulator and a reference clock.

029

Detection limit

The lowest signal or concentration that a specified method can reliably distinguish from its blank under stated conditions.

030

DLIA

Digital lock-in amplifier — a digital method that extracts a signal at a known reference frequency while rejecting other noise.

031

DRC

Design-rule check — software checks whether a PCB layout follows specified spacing and manufacturing rules.

032

Drosophila

A genus of flies. The fruit fly Drosophila melanogaster is a source of well-studied odorant receptors and inspired the project's sensing and coding designs.

033

Electronic nose

An instrument that combines chemical sensors and pattern analysis to distinguish odor mixtures.

034

EMA

Exponential moving average — a display smoothing method that gives more weight to recent values. Smoothing does not remove systematic measurement error.

035

EMCV IRES

An internal ribosome entry site derived from encephalomyocarditis virus, used to translate a downstream protein from the same mRNA.

036

ERC

Electrical-rule check — software checks for possible connection mistakes in a circuit schematic.

037

ESP32

A family of microcontrollers that can control electronics and process data on a compact board.

038

Excitation light

Light used to stimulate a fluorescent reporter; it must be separated from the weaker light emitted by the reporter.

039

F1 score

A classification score combining precision and recall. Its meaning depends on the dataset and evaluation split.

040

False negative

A test misses a problem that the reference condition says is present.

041

False positive

A test flags a problem when the reference condition says that problem is absent.

042

FDM

Frequency-division multiplexing — assigning distinct carrier frequencies so multiple optical channels can share a detector path.

043

FITI

Taiwan's From IP to IPO innovation and entrepreneurship program, which provided business-plan and pitch feedback to the team.

044

Fluorescence

Light emitted by a molecule after it absorbs light at another wavelength. AeroSense uses a calcium-sensitive fluorescent reporter in its sensing design.

045

Fluorescence quantum yield

The ratio of emitted fluorescence photons to absorbed excitation photons. It describes emission efficiency, not how much light the detector collects.

046

G418

An antibiotic used to select mammalian cells carrying a compatible resistance gene.

047

GC–MS

Gas chromatography–mass spectrometry — a laboratory method that separates volatile compounds and helps identify them by mass.

048

GCaMP

A family of genetically encoded fluorescent reporters that become brighter when calcium inside a cell rises.

049

GCaMP6

A genetically encoded fluorescent calcium indicator used to report intracellular Ca²⁺ changes.

050

GPIO

General-purpose input/output — programmable microcontroller pins that read or drive digital signals.

051

Headspace

The gas above a liquid or solid sample; volatile molecules from the sample may enter this space.

052

HEK293

A human cell line derived from embryonic kidney culture and transformed with adenovirus 5 DNA. HEK293 is the host used for the team’s heterologous receptor-expression experiments.

053

High impedance

A large opposition to current flow. In a photodiode receiver, high-impedance input nodes require careful layout because small leakage currents can interfere with the measured signal.

054

I/Q demodulation

Comparing a measured signal with two reference waves separated by a quarter cycle. Their in-phase and quadrature components describe the signal's amplitude and phase at a selected frequency.

055

iGEM Registry

The shared database where teams document biological parts, including their identity, sequence, and characterization.

056

Indole

A volatile organic compound that can occur in microbial metabolism; a signal of indole alone cannot identify a pathogen.

057

Integrated Human Practices

The way stakeholder input changes a project's choices throughout development, with the change and its evidence documented.

058

Ionomycin

A compound that raises intracellular calcium and can test whether a calcium reporter responds independently of the odorant-receptor channel.

059

IRB

Institutional Review Board — a body that reviews certain research involving human participants; whether review is required depends on the activity and local rules.

060

IRES

Internal ribosome entry site — an RNA element that can initiate translation of a downstream open reading frame independently of the 5′ cap.

061

ISO/IEC 17025

An international standard for the competence of testing and calibration laboratories. A future certification goal is not current accreditation.

062

KC

Kenyon cell — a mushroom-body neuron that typically participates in sparse, high-dimensional odor representations.

063

KiCad

Open-source software for designing circuit schematics and printed circuit boards.

064

Kozak sequence

A short sequence around a start codon that helps a mammalian ribosome begin protein translation.

065

Lateral inhibition

A circuit motif in which active units suppress neighbors, often increasing contrast between signals.

066

LC–MS

Liquid chromatography–mass spectrometry — a laboratory method that separates compounds in a liquid sample and analyzes their masses.

067

LDO

Low-dropout regulator — a circuit that maintains a regulated supply voltage with a small required voltage difference between input and output.

068

LED

Light-emitting diode — a semiconductor light source. In the reader, blue excitation light stimulates a fluorescent response; it is not the fluorescence signal itself.

069

Life-cycle assessment

A method for estimating environmental impacts across a product's materials, manufacture, use, and disposal.

070

LOI

Letter of intent — a written statement that an organization is interested in a defined next step; it is not the same as a purchase.

071

LOSO

Leave-one-sensor-out analysis — remove one sensor at a time and see how a model's performance changes.

072

LSH

Locality-sensitive hashing — a method that maps similar inputs to nearby codes with high probability.

073

MB

Mushroom body — an insect brain structure involved in olfactory learning, memory, and sparse odor coding.

074

MBON

Mushroom body output neuron — a neuron that reads mushroom-body activity and contributes to behavioral decisions.

075

mCherry

A red fluorescent protein used here as an expression marker; it is distinct from the calcium-sensitive sensing readout.

076

MCU

Microcontroller unit — a small computer on a chip that coordinates electronics, timing, and data acquisition.

077

Monte Carlo simulation

A numerical method that follows many randomly sampled events. In the optical model, simulated photon paths estimate how much light reaches the detector.

078

MVP

Minimum viable product — the smallest usable product that can test a specific customer need.

079

Mycotoxin

A toxic secondary metabolite that some fungi can produce. Detecting mold growth is not the same measurement as quantifying a mycotoxin.

080

NeoR

A gene giving resistance to neomycin-family selection drugs such as G418, used to select cells carrying an expression vector.

081

OBP

Odorant-binding protein — a soluble protein that can bind and help transport hydrophobic odorants.

082

Odorant receptor (OR)

An insect receptor protein that helps turn binding by an odor molecule into a cellular signal, usually in partnership with Orco.

083

Optical fluence

Light energy incident per unit area, integrated over time. In a steady optical calculation, the corresponding fluence rate describes light power per unit area arriving from all directions.

084

Or49b

A fruit-fly odorant receptor considered in the project's receptor library for indole-related sensing research.

085

Orco

Odorant receptor co-receptor — an insect co-receptor that partners with ORs to form ligand-gated ion channels.

086

Part collection

A coordinated set of biological parts with a clear purpose and documentation showing how the parts work together.

087

PCA

Principal component analysis — a method that summarizes variation in many measurements using a smaller set of combined axes.

088

PCB

Printed circuit board — the physical board that connects electronic components. A CAD layout is a board design, not proof that a board was manufactured.

089

pcDNA3.1(+) plasmid

A mammalian expression plasmid that carries inserted DNA and regulatory elements needed for expression in cultured cells.

090

PDMS

Polydimethylsiloxane — a silicone polymer considered in transport models. It is not part of the current AeroSense assembly or required bill of materials.

091

Photodiode

An electronic component that converts incoming light into a small electrical current.

092

Photodiode responsivity

Photocurrent produced per unit of incident optical power, usually expressed in amperes per watt. It depends on the wavelength of the light.

093

Plasmid

A circular DNA molecule used as a carrier for genes or other genetic elements in an experiment.

094

PN

Projection neuron — a neuron that relays processed olfactory information from the antennal lobe to higher centers.

095

POC

Proof of concept — an early demonstration that a core idea can work under defined conditions.

096

Poly(A) signal

A DNA signal that directs formation of the 3′ end and poly(A) tail of a messenger RNA in a eukaryotic cell.

097

RFC 10

An iGEM BioBrick assembly standard. Compatibility with it must be checked independently of other standards.

098

RFC1000

iGEM Registry assembly standard for composing BioBrick/RFC-compatible parts with defined prefix and suffix sites.

099

Risk group

A classification of biological agents by the relative risk they pose; institutional assignment must be confirmed locally.

100

Risk tier

A category such as low, medium, or high used to guide the next action; it is not a direct measurement of food safety.

101

SAM

Serviceable available market — the part of TAM the product could realistically serve given its scope and geography.

102

Sanger sequencing

A DNA sequencing method often used to verify a cloned sequence or junction.

103

SAR ADC

Successive-approximation analog-to-digital converter — a circuit that samples a voltage and resolves its digital value by successive comparisons.

104

Screening

An early check that identifies samples needing closer attention. A screening result is not a certified safety verdict.

105

SDG

United Nations Sustainable Development Goal — a shared framework for describing social and environmental aims, not proof that an aim has been achieved.

106

SDS

Safety Data Sheet — a supplier document describing chemical hazards, handling, and emergency information for a substance.

107

Sensitivity

In electronics, how strongly a device responds to a small input. In classification, the proportion of true positives correctly detected. The intended meaning needs context.

108

SNN

Spiking neural network — a neural model that communicates with discrete spike events over time.

109

SNR

Signal-to-noise ratio — how large the useful signal is compared with unwanted variation or background.

110

SOM

Serviceable obtainable market — the share a team could plausibly capture within a stated period and sales plan.

111

SOP

Standard operating procedure — an institution-approved written method for performing a task safely and consistently.

112

Sparse coding

A representation strategy in which only a small fraction of units are strongly active for a given input.

113

Specificity

In sensing, how selectively a system responds to a target. In classification, the proportion of true negatives correctly identified.

114

SPI

Serial Peripheral Interface — a synchronous serial bus commonly used between a microcontroller and peripherals such as ADCs.

115

StandardScaler

A preprocessing step that centers features and scales them by their training-set standard deviation; it must be fitted without test-set leakage.

116

STDP

Spike-timing-dependent plasticity — a learning rule that changes connection strength based on the timing of neuron-like spikes.

117

STL

A triangle-mesh file format commonly used to transfer a three-dimensional part to 3D-printing software. A printable model is not evidence of validated fit or function.

118

SVM

Support vector machine — a model that learns a boundary between classes from labeled examples.

119

t90

The time needed for a response to reach 90% of its final change. Gas-phase, liquid-phase, and biological response times describe different processes.

120

TAM

Total addressable market — the broadest plausible market for a defined product, under stated assumptions.

121

TIA

Transimpedance amplifier — a circuit that converts a small input current into a usable output voltage.

122

UART

Universal asynchronous receiver-transmitter — a serial data interface. The AeroSense programming connection uses 3.3 V UART; it is separate from USB-C power.

123

VOC

Volatile organic compound — a carbon-containing chemical that readily enters the gas phase at ambient conditions.

124

VREF

Voltage reference — a stable comparison voltage that helps an ADC convert an input signal into numbers.

125

VUAA1

A synthetic compound used to activate Orco-containing insect odorant-receptor channels; useful as a pathway control.

126

Willingness to pay

How much a specified buyer would pay for a defined solution; it must be tested with the actual buyer, not inferred from general interest.

127

ΔF/F₀

Relative fluorescence change — the change in fluorescence divided by a baseline fluorescence level.
