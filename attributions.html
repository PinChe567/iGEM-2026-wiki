External engineering and synthesis support

iST Group

iST provided professional schematic and PCB-layout review and assisted with the second design review and manufacturing preparation. The feedback covered the LMP7721 pin mapping and guard, photodiode pads, TIA placement, ADC and reference decoupling, SAR input behavior, and ESP32 EN/BOOT circuitry. The team’s resulting design decisions are recorded in Engineering. This support is distinct from measured reader performance.

Gene synthesis and partner acknowledgments

The experiment source identifies GenScript as the plasmid-synthesis provider. Vendor delivery and in-silico sequence checking are distinct from sequence verification of the team’s individual colonies. See Experiments and Parts for the documented construct workflow. Sponsor logos acknowledge support; a logo alone is not a claim that a company designed or validated AeroSense.

Responsible use of AI

AI tools assisted website implementation, editing, code-based analysis, and the creation of conceptual illustrations. Codex was used for website revisions and analysis scripts; OpenAI image generation was used for editorial artwork. A Claude review supplied by the team informed the October 2026 scientific and usability revisions.

What these tools can and cannot establish

Generated artwork is explanatory, not an experimental observation. Microscopy analysis starts from team-supplied image files; the raw files are retained and analysis choices, exclusions, and limitations are documented. AI-assisted code and statistical calculations do not create biological replication, verify a cell’s viability, establish instrument calibration, or supply missing experimental records. The data package exposes the analysis for inspection rather than treating a generated conclusion as evidence.

The team retains responsibility for scientific claims, source permissions, attributions, and the final submission. Unresolved source and validation gaps are identified on their respective pages.

Prior work and our additions

AeroSense builds on published OR/Orco cellular assays [1], the Orco agonist VUAA1 [2], and fly-inspired similarity search [3]. These are scientific foundations, not team inventions. The Description and Contribution pages distinguish inherited principles from the team’s construct choices, reader design, microscopy analysis, and adaptation lessons.

Software, images, and reuse

Image provenance

Team photographs and supplied microscopy are separate from editorial illustrations. Scientific plots identify their source as measurements, simulations, or illustrative calculations. Repeated plots refer to the same dataset or simulation, not independent replication. The conceptual fruit-fly and reader drawings explain the design; they do not document fabrication or measured anatomy.

The definition-orb geometry adapts Jakub Antalik’s thinking-orbs under its MIT license. Third-party source headers and distribution notices remain with the relevant files; bundled software notices collect the locally available notices.

Reuse terms

Downloadable files are made available for inspection and reproducibility. A separate license for the team’s code and hardware has not yet been selected in the supplied records; this page does not assign rights to partner materials or promise unrestricted reuse. Preserve the third-party licenses that accompany their files.

Trace the work

The Notebook records dated work and explicitly separates undated provenance records. The Engineering page explains how reviews changed the design, while Members provides the same team-supplied contribution statements in one place.
