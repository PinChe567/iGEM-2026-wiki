AI boundaries

Team verification required

The intended project boundary is that AI must not be used to generate scientific data, data figures, gels, microscopy images, experimental results, stakeholder quotes, or citations.

This boundary statement becomes an official team claim only after a named reviewer confirms it against actual practice. [TEAM CONTENT REQUIRED: verification date and reviewer]

Until that confirmation, treat the sentence above as a design rule for wiki work, not as a verified historical fact about every tool the team may have used offline.
