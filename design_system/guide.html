DESIGN GUIDE · NTHU iGEM 2026

About this design direction

Keep the large dark covers, current illustrated cards and authored interactions. Use framed chapters, gentle local tints, white evidence interiors and clear sources. Local Fraunces gives the story warmth; system sans gives the research clarity. This restoration supersedes the flat-white and minimal drafts.

This guide reuses existing editorial artwork and labeled schematic assets. It introduces no experimental measurements and does not change Notion or publish the wiki.

07

Let the story move.
Keep reading in control.

Preserve authored journeys, product opening and useful scientific interactions. Animation never gates access to the argument.
