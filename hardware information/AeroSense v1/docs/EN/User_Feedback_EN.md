Observer logs damage, reversed parts, incomplete seating, missing washers, overtightening, tube strain and help requests. Predefine completion: all parts present/oriented, wells removable, connectors accessible and no visible mechanical load. Appearance alone is not completion.

Analysis: report participant backgrounds, completion rates, median/IQR time and error/help categories. Report per-item Likert distributions; N/A is not neutral. A five-novice pilot is formative, not proof of broad usability. Retest improvements with new participants to reduce learning effects.

For the wiki, trace feedback → design decision → retest result. Retain negative feedback and do not fabricate satisfaction, participant counts or questionnaire results. Anonymize quotations before publication.
