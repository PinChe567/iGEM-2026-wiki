# Best Hardware evidence matrix

Checked 2026-10-05. This matrix paraphrases the four official Best Hardware ballot topics; it does not invent a separate Overgrad rubric. The published criteria cover synthetic-biology need, user feedback, hardware proof of concept and reproducibility. [Official iGEM Special Awards page](https://competition.igem.org/judging/special-prizes)

| Official topic | AeroSense question | Evidence to publish | Claim boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Need in synthetic biology | Who needs repeatable portable receptor/GCaMP measurements, and why? | Need→specification→design mapping; current workflow limitations; sourced comparisons | Measure size/mass and state complete costs; do not merely call it cheap or portable |
| 2 User testing and learning | Can another person build/use it, and what did feedback change? | Anonymous records from 3–5 users; issue→change→two retests; before/after pages | Blank forms are a plan, not results; describe the small sample honestly |
| 3 Functional hardware POC | Can the PCB acquire, separate frequencies, detect a standard and remain repeatable? | Real waveforms, blank/three-level plot, single-well matrix, five reassemblies | Simulation/compilation do not replace device data; a dye standard does not validate cellular odor detection |
| 4 Reproduction by another team | Can others obtain, print, assemble, flash, calibrate and analyze it? | Final STL/parametric CAD, categorized BOM, illustrated manual, firmware/UI, raw data and replay commands | Disclose uncertain component dimensions, material validation and unquoted costs |

## Two engineering cycles

**Enclosure cycle:** identify the earlier interface/fan/optical-shield/seal-compression/flow-direction problems; show the redesigned geometry and simulations; then add actual fit, room-light and reassembly results. If a separate compression plate is needed, explain the extra component's sealing function rather than removing necessary functionality to advertise a lower part count.

**Manual cycle:** give the instructions to people uninvolved in design; record the actual sticking points; revise figures, labels or sequence; retest the same difficult tasks. Report received feedback, not anticipated satisfaction. Repeated participants have learning effects.

## Appropriate claim levels

- Simulation without hardware data supports a prototype-testing direction, not verified device performance.
- Passing cell-free optical/electrical tests supports a cell-free hardware POC; biology and odor classification may remain unvalidated.
- Independent biological samples with suitable controls support only the response scope actually tested. Multiple blocks from one well are technical repeats.
- Without real labeled data and batch-separated evaluation, do not report neural-network classification accuracy.

Consult the current [iGEM Judge Handbook](https://competition.igem.org/judging/handbook) before publishing. This matrix organizes evidence; it does not promise an award.
