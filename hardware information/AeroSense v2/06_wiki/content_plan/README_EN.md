Prepared 2026-10-05. These are unpublished editorial drafts, not new experiments or a replacement for the complete project wiki. Existing Engineering Cycles 1–3 remain historical records. The live pages were retrieved successfully by the parent workflow; their exact snapshots and HTTP/hash metadata are in `../../08_reference/site_context/current_pages/`. GitHub default-branch files were independently read and matched those pages after newline normalization.

- `Placement_Plan_EN.md`: where evidence belongs, actions in priority order, award relevance and archive boundaries.
- `Hardware_Sections_EN.md`: paste-ready additions/replacements for Hardware.
- `Model_Sections_EN.md`: paste-ready physical-model sections; preserve the separate SNN benchmark.
- `Description_Sections_EN.md`: concise system-level wording and current evidence boundary.
- `Docking_OBP_Evidence_EN.md`: what can honestly be published before docking records and exact protein identity are supplied.
- `Appendix_Contents_EN.md`: four-archive extraction, detailed gas-model boundaries and the counterfactual liquid sensitivity.
- `primary_refs.json`: primary references, verification dates and year-specific limitations.
- `page_audit.json`: inspected pages, anchors, source provenance and docking search scope.

Each document has a Traditional Chinese `_ZH.md` counterpart. Links resolve to local package files or another draft in this folder. `page_audit.json` maps those drafts and anchors to the intended website pages and official 2026 standard routes. Rewrite these local paths during integration; they are not ready-made official upload URLs. The parent integration workflow owns image/video/download URLs and the appendix index; no upload or publication has occurred here.

Use one canonical dataset for each displayed number. Do not combine the old public page's geometry/maps with the current model, and do not copy old optional STL links into the selected print set.
