Meet Team NTHU

AeroSense is built by students at National Tsing Hua University who connect living-cell sensing, fly-inspired computation, and portable optical hardware—with care for evidence, safety, and the people who will read this work.

Names and portraits await team-verified English records

One project, many disciplines

AeroSense is one platform with several intertwined functions. People may hold more than one responsibility; roles below describe workstreams, not a fixed org chart.

  • Wet lab — constructs, cell culture, agonist/VOC assays, and biological controls.
  • Dry lab / model — fluorescence feature pipelines, fly-inspired transforms, evaluation, and documentation on Model.
  • Hardware — optical path, electronics, firmware, and bench validation on Hardware.
  • Human practices / education — stakeholder engagement, safety framing, and teaching materials.
  • Design / wiki — information architecture, figures, and public documentation quality.
  • Coordination — scheduling, cross-track handoffs, and judging readiness.
