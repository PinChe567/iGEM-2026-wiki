Sustainability

Sustainability is not a badge. It is a set of trade-offs we can trace.

AeroSense could create value by changing how food-risk information reaches a decision — earlier triage instead of late visual calls or slow destructive assays. The same system carries environmental, economic, and social costs: living-cell consumables, electronics, access, and the risk of over-trust. This page separates what we have measured from what stakeholders told us, what we estimated, what we designed, what we still only hypothesize, and what remains an open gap.

  • Scope B Longer-term commercial hypothesis — Taiwan banana export disease-detection candidate beachhead; mango / tea as follow-ons. Not a completed field result.

03 · System

Define the system boundary

Sustainability questions only make sense inside a named system. AeroSense sits between a biological odor signal and a human decision about food — with different stakes depending on whether we are talking about the iGEM track or a commercial beachhead hypothesis.

Inside the boundary

  • Living OR/Orco + reporter cell cartridge, ESP32 FDM/DLIA reader, neuromorphic classification stack.
  • Wet-lab consumables, culture media, and single-use plastics used to build and test the sensing layer.
  • Agricultural institutions already interviewed (stations, associations, facility farms) and the confirmatory lab chain a screen is meant to feed.
  • Desired buyers in Scope B: exporters / packing houses — not yet interviewed directly.

Outside the boundary (for now)

  • Clinical diagnosis or medical-device claims.
  • Mycotoxin certification or definitive food-safety verdicts.
  • Sealed field pilots, contracted LOIs, or measured shipment-loss reduction.
  • A completed product LCA or priced manufacturing COGS.
Two scopes share a sensing stack. They do not share completed results.

A short lens — not the page opener

Sustainability problems couple ecological, economic, and social systems over time. A change that improves one link (earlier disease triage) can burden another (cartridge waste, unequal access, false-negative overtrust).

We use the UN Sustainable Development Goals as a shared vocabulary that iGEM teams and reviewers already know. They are useful for locating trade-offs; they are not evidence that our solution is sustainable.

Classic definitions (Brundtland; three-pillar framings; systems primers) sit in References. AeroSense stays at the center of this page.

05 · Pathways

Our four primary impact pathways

Each pathway uses the same logic: Target → Problem → AeroSense mechanism → Evidence → Boundary / risk → Next measurement. Empty evidence stays empty.

SDG 2 — Zero Hunger

Scope B primary

  1. Target

    Target 2.4 — more resilient agricultural practices along the supply chain. Target 2.3 — smallholder productivity and incomes — is the tension we refuse to hide.

  2. Problem

    Symptom-only visual sampling is late; PCR / ELISA / GC–MS are accurate but slow and destructive for every-shipment use. Team desk estimate frames Taiwan banana fungal-disease-related risk at roughly NT$3.51 billion/year (~33.6% of NT$10.445 billion sector output) — sector framing, not a shipment ledger.

    • Estimated

  3. Mechanism

    Designed VOC risk tiers that could inform isolate / reroute / time-shipment decisions before symptoms are obvious. This is an aim — not a demonstrated loss reduction.

    • Hypothesis
    • Designed

  4. Evidence

    Agricultural-institution interviews support where pain is described on the ground. No measured food-loss reduction. No banana/mango exporter interview on record.

    • Stakeholder-backed
    • Open gap

  5. Boundary / risk

    A tool that improves exporter resilience can still fail Target 2.3 if value never reaches smallholders — or if individual farmers resist a device that flags their own product.

  6. Next measurement

    Exporter / packing-house interviews on batch-level loss volumes and willingness to pay; trace whether association buyers pass protection downstream.

SDG 9 — Industry, Innovation & Infrastructure

Shared stack

  1. Target

    Target 9.5 — enhance research and industrial technological capability. Target 9.b — support domestic technology development.

  2. Problem

    External e-nose industry framing: the hard part is the sensing element — selectivity, sensitivity, lifetime, reproducibility — not electronics or algorithms alone.

    • Stakeholder-backed

  3. Mechanism

    Living Drosophila OR/Orco + GCaMP reporter cells as a swappable sensing element; team-designed ESP32-standalone four-channel FDM/DLIA reader; neuromorphic classification with a published research lineage through advisors (Cheng, Charng, et al., Science Advances, 2025). Portable architecture is intended — not field-validated.

    • Designed

  4. Evidence

    Paper lineage is published open-access research (product accuracy ≠ paper results). Hardware V2 layout completed 11 September 2026 per Hardware documentation; fabrication and the validation ladder remain pending. Power consumption is not measured — we do not claim <1 W.

    • Designed
    • Open gap

  5. Boundary / risk

    Living cells add biosafety and consumable burden. IP that is only “planned / in preparation” could later restrict which buyers get access (see SDG 10). Extra-lab live-cell use remains restricted on the Safety page.

  6. Next measurement

    Hardware validation ladder (bring-up → dark → leakage → fluorescence standard → FDM/DLIA → live-cell); measured reader power; net footprint including biology cold-chain.

SDG 12 — Responsible Consumption & Production

Scope A + B

  1. Target

    12.3 food loss/waste; 12.5 waste reduction through reuse; 12.4 sound management of chemicals and wastes — including our own.

  2. Problem

    Late discovery can discard whole batches; over-caution can discard good food; destructive assays consume sample and reagents per test.

  3. Mechanism

    Designed as non-destructive headspace screening with modular cartridges intended for multiple readings before replacement, and risk-tier outputs rather than a binary discard signal. Recyclable or biodegradable housing and a simple LCA are planned — not completed.

    • Designed
    • Hypothesis

  4. Evidence

    Education publishes directional n = 28 counts (including false-negative intolerance). A “100% whole-package discard” line appears in some team syntheses but is not currently tabulated on Education — marked for verification. Cartridge reuse and lifetime advantage versus conventional sensors are not measured. “Near-daily replacement” for some commercial sensors is industry paraphrase (context-specific), not a universal e-nose fact.

    • Measured
    • Open gap

  5. Boundary / risk

    Living-cell cartridges are themselves consumables. “Self-renewing cells ⇒ lower replacement frequency” remains a hypothesis until lifetime and LCA exist. We do not claim a net environmental benefit.

  6. Next measurement

    Reuse-cycle protocol with n; simple LCA; wet-lab plastics and hazard-class inventory; verify or retire the 100% discard survey item against the instrument.

SDG 3 — Good Health & Well-being

Scope A discipline

  1. Target

    Health-protective claim discipline for any food-adjacent screen.

  2. Problem

    A mold VOC is not automatically a food-safety or mycotoxin verdict.

  3. Mechanism

    Position AeroSense as rapid risk screening that recommends confirmatory lab testing — never a diagnostic replacement. Risk-tier outputs, informed by survey false-negative intolerance.

  4. Evidence

    Chia-Nien interview paraphrase; Safety page laboratory-scope boundary; Education FN counts (directional).

    • Stakeholder-backed
    • Designed

  5. Boundary / risk

    AeroSense does not claim mycotoxin detection. Overtrust of a “green” tier remains a safety failure mode.

  6. Next measurement

    Confirmatory-escalation UX tests; keep non-claims visible in operator materials.

SDG 10 — Reduced Inequalities

Access

  1. Target

    Avoid locking screening capability to only the best-resourced buyers.

  2. Problem

    Individual-farmer pricing can exclude smallholders and may face adoption resistance.

  3. Mechanism

    Current design response: sell or pilot through associations, cooperatives, stations, and certification-adjacent bodies.

  4. Evidence

    Analytical-instrumentation interview. Outcome equity is not validated.

    • Stakeholder-backed
    • Open gap

  5. Boundary / risk

    Intention is not an equitable outcome. Restrictive licensing later could recreate the same exclusion from the IP side.

  6. Next measurement

    Pricing and distribution pilots that record who actually gets access.

09 · Footprint

Our own footprint

Before claiming we reduce other people’s waste, we account for what AeroSense itself consumes. This is a system-boundary inventory — not a completed life-cycle assessment. Where mass, energy, or hazard tallies do not exist, we write Not yet quantified, not zeros.

No formal LCA has been completed for AeroSense. We do not claim a net environmental benefit.

Simple system boundary

Status labels mark what is designed or known in kind versus what is quantified. Quantified = a published measurement exists. Not yet quantified = known stream, no number on this wiki.

Flow: Inputs → AeroSense process → Outputs

Inputs

  • Living-cell culture / media Designed Not yet quantified

    HEK293T OR/Orco + reporter culture; media and biosafety handling required for the biological sensing layer.

  • Plastics / consumables Open gap Not yet quantified

    Wet-lab plastics and disposables generated during construct development and testing.

  • Sensing cartridge materials Designed Not yet quantified

    Modular headspace cartridge architecture intended; material mass and housing polymer not selected / not weighed.

  • Electronics Designed Not yet quantified

    ESP32-standalone V2 reader layout and portable power path on Hardware; BOM mass and e-waste not tallied.

    Reader power draw and lab equipment energy not measured. Older “<1 W” wording is retired until bench data exist.

  • Storage / transport requirements Hypothesis Not yet quantified

    Biological layer likely needs culture / cold-chain conditions metal-oxide sensors do not — magnitude unknown.
