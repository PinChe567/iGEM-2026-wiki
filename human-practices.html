Integrated Human Practices

How society shaped AeroSense—and how AeroSense may affect society

Structure ready · no verified engagement records published yet

Our responsibility question

Is our project responsible and good for the world?

AeroSense is a research prototype that pairs living-cell olfactory receptors with fly-inspired computation and portable optical readout to interpret volatile organic compounds (VOCs) patterns (see Description · Intended context and boundaries). Human Practices exists to test whether that work stays responsible: who could benefit, who could be burdened, what we must not claim, and which design choices should change because of those answers.

Dimension Current team record
Primary application context Strategic open decision. Candidate domains discussed in team materials include agricultural, environmental, food, and medical VOC contexts. A primary 2026 use case is not finalized on Description / Design.
Affected communities (intended mapping) Depends on the chosen domain (growers/inspectors, environmental monitors, food-quality workers, clinicians/researchers, and the public who may see VOC claims). No community has a published engagement record yet.
Intended benefit A documented, contained research path toward more interpretable bio-digital odor sensing—not a validated diagnostic, food-safety certificate, or field-certified device (see Description · context boundaries).
Major uncertainty Whether receptor panels, assays, models, and hardware can support any chosen domain without overclaiming; whether living-cell cartridges are operable and containable outside shared labs; who would maintain calibration and interpret uncertain outputs.

This page will not invent stakeholders, meetings, quotes, survey results, consent, insights, or design changes. Empty sections stay empty until the team supplies dated evidence.

Ethical analysis

The analysis below is the team’s responsibility framing for a living-cell VOC sensing platform. It is not derived from completed stakeholder interviews. Each row should later cite engagement or institutional advice when available.

Issue Why it applies to AeroSense Current mitigation / stance Evidence status
Living-cell device responsibility HEK293T cartridges raise containment, viability, and waste duties beyond inert sensors Contained-use framing; Safety inventory Planned controls · not field-proven
False positives / negatives Pattern classifiers can mis-rank VOC conditions; harm depends on domain Require baselines, uncertainty, and refuse domain claims without validation (Model / Results) No domain error costs engaged yet
Access and affordability “Portable” or “low cost” claims can exclude users if BOM/maintenance are opaque Hardware refuses cost/portability claims without dated evidence No priced BOM / use test
Data interpretation Outputs may be over-read as diagnoses or certificates Description boundaries; provenance flags for model/hardware handoff UX for uncertainty not designed with users
Environmental release / containment Engineered cells and VOC reagents must not become a release pathway Safety · Biosecurity and dual-use stance Institutional SOP details pending
Misuse Documentation could be stretched toward harmful VOC scenarios Emphasize contained research methods; omit actionable misuse detail Publication review process pending team checklist
Overdiagnosis / overclaiming Medical or safety narratives are especially easy to oversell Primary domain still open; clinical/food/field claims withheld Open strategic decision
Maintenance burden Who recalibrates optics, replaces cartridges, and interprets drift? Named as Hardware/HP gap; no operator study Unresolved

What we could not resolve

  • Stakeholder gaps: zero published engagements across biosafety officers, domain experts, affected publics, and operators.
  • Conflicting needs: not yet mapped (e.g. portability vs containment, early medical narrative vs humility) because domain choice and interviews are missing.
  • Limitations of this page: structure and responsibility framing only; cannot demonstrate Silver/Best Integrated HP integration until cases exist.
  • Next steps: freeze or explicitly defer primary application; run method checklist; file consent; publish timeline rows; close at least one insight→change→test loop linked to Design/Engineering/Hardware/Model; expand Education without overclaiming.
